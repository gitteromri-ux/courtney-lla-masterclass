SCRIPT 06 · FOUNDING-FACULTY FILM — "THE $2M ANSWER"
FRAMEWORK French Atelier · 60–75s · direct to camera

She Out-Aged a $2-Million-a-Year Man — on an Ordinary Budget

World-before-face cold open, teacher reveal, the two factors, proof, offer, and a mirrored brand-lock-up close — all in Julie's documented voice.

Act I · World before face — cold open

"Somewhere right now, a man is spending two million dollars a year trying to age slower. Cold plunges. A hundred pills a day. A full-time medical team. And for more than a year, on the same scoreboard that ranks him — he was in second place. First place was a working mother, living on an ordinary budget."

ON SCREEN: $2,000,000 a year. And he came in second.

Act II · Teacher reveal — to camera

"That mother is me. I'm Julie Gibson Clark. For over a year I ranked second in the world for slowing my own aging — ahead of the men with the labs and the millions. I didn't beat them with money. I beat them with the boring things, done every single day."

Act III · The two factors

"There are two factors that decide almost everything: how consistent you are, and how well you actually read your own body. Not the plunge. Not the pills. Consistency, and your own numbers."

Act IV · Proof — word-to-image cutaway

"My pace of aging is measured at point-six-six-five — my body is aging about a third slower than the calendar. On an ordinary budget. He spends two million a year."

CARD: DunedinPACE 0.665 · aging ~34% slower · ordinary budget vs $2M/yr

Act V · The offer

"That's why I teach at Longevity Life Academy — the two factors, across six simple pillars: eat, sleep, move, supplement, protect your heart, and read your own data. Eighteen live sessions that turn a millionaire's obsession into something you can actually do."

Act VI · Close — mirror the open

"The biggest bang for your buck was never expensive — it's eating right, moving, and sleeping, done every day. Second place cost me nothing but consistency. Join us, and design the life ahead of you."

Welcome & Brand Lock-Up · Julie Gibson Clark, LLA Founding Faculty
"I'm Julie — welcome to Longevity Life Academy."
Bookend close mirrors the open. #2 ranking stated PAST-tense ("was," "for over a year"). No "$12" figure used — affordability carried by "ordinary budget."

SCRIPT 07 · FOUNDING-FACULTY FILM — "SECOND PLACE, FIRST TRUTH"
FRAMEWORK French Atelier · 60–75s · direct to camera

Second in the World — and That's Exactly Why You Can Trust It

The humility flip: coming second, not first, is the proof — because she didn't buy the result, she built it. Same six-act arc, same brand lock-up.

Act I · World before face — the scoreboard

"There's a global ranking for how slowly people are aging. At the top, the names you'd expect — labs, money, full-time teams. And for over a year, just below them, in second place, was a name nobody recognized. No lab. No fortune. Just a woman who paid attention."

Act II · Teacher reveal — humility flip

"I'm that name. Julie Gibson Clark. And honestly? I'm glad I came second, not first. Because first place had unlimited money. I didn't. Which means everything I did — you can do too."

Act III · The two factors

"It came down to two things. Consistency — boring habits kept every single day. And visibility — actually reading your own body instead of guessing. You don't need a fortune. You need a curriculum."

Act IV · Proof

"My body aging about a third slower than the calendar. Holding second in the world for more than a year — on the habits, not the budget."

CARD: DunedinPACE 0.665 · #2 held 1+ year · habits, not budget

Act V · The offer

"At Longevity Life Academy: eighteen live sessions across the six pillars, and your own numbers turned into a blueprint. Not a millionaire's protocol. A learnable path."

Act VI · Close — emotional dwell

"You don't need to come first. You need to start. It's your life. Live it well. Join us."

Welcome & Brand Lock-Up · Julie Gibson Clark, LLA Founding Faculty
"I'm Julie — welcome to Longevity Life Academy."
